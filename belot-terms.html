1. The service

Belot Score is a free score pad with advertising. You play the cards at the table. The app records numbers you enter.

Bile are stored as typed. BT 1 and BT 2 count as 0. BT 3 counts as −10 and the count returns to BT 1. A separate −10 subtracts 10. The target starts at 101 or 51 and rises by 50 when two or more columns pass it. If that does not match the count you agreed at the table, the table’s count prevails. The app is not a referee.
