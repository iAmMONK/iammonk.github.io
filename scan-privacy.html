Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy describes how ScanTidy (the “App”), provided by Cristian Lungu, handles information. The App is an offline document scanner. It does not require an account and does not operate a backend that stores your documents.

Also see the Terms and the Imprint.

Information kept on your device

Pages you scan or import, text recognized from those pages, file names, and the library of documents stay in the App’s storage on your device. ScanTidy does not upload that content to a server I control.

Text recognition uses an on-device model. The document scanner is provided by Google Play services and also runs on the device. Play services may download that scanner module, which needs a network connection the first time it is fetched. After that, scanning and text recognition do not send your pages to me.

If you share or open a saved file, Android and the app you choose handle that file under their own terms. That transfer happens only when you start it.

Backup

Android backup and device transfer may include the App’s preferences, such as your chosen folder template and appearance settings. The document files and the App database are excluded from backup and from device-to-device transfer.

App lock

If you turn on app lock, the App asks Android to confirm it is you. The fingerprint or face data stays with the system. The App does not receive or store a biometric template.

Other people’s information

A scan can contain personal data about someone else, such as a name on a letter or an identity document. You decide what to scan and who can see the file. I do not receive those documents.

What this App does not do

There is no account, no advertising, and no sale of your information. The App does not request your contacts, location, or microphone. A network permission may still appear because the Play services scanner library declares it. The App does not use that permission to send your documents anywhere.

Retention and deletion

Delete a document in the App to remove that document and its pages from the device. Uninstalling the App removes the App and the documents stored inside it. Copies you already saved to a folder you chose, or shared with another app, are not deleted by uninstalling.
