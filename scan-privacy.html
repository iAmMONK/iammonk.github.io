Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy describes how ScanTidy (the “App”), provided by Cristian Lungu, handles information. The App is an on-device document scanner. It does not require an account and does not operate a backend that stores your documents.

Information that stays on your device

Pages you scan, text recognized from those pages, and file names stay on your device. Recognition uses an on-device Latin text model. I do not upload your documents, recognized text, or file names to a server I control, and I do not sell that information.

Google Play services may download the on-device scanner module the first time you scan. That download is the scanner software, not your documents. A networking permission can appear in the installed App because that scanner library merges it. The App does not use it to upload your documents.

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, or microphone.

Backup

Android backup excludes your document files and the App’s database (scantidy.db). Preferences, such as theme, language, and save settings, may be included in backup. Document images and recognized text are not.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, the App asks the system to confirm it is you, using the biometric or device-credential prompt already set up on the device. The App does not receive or store a fingerprint, face template, or device PIN.

Other people’s information

A scan can contain personal data about other people, such as a letter, an invoice, or an identity document. You are responsible for having the right to capture that material and for how you keep or share it.

Sharing

If you share or export a document, that transfer is yours. The system share sheet and the app you send the file to handle it under their own terms.

Children

The App is not directed at children. It does not create an account and it does not show ads.

Retention and deletion

Delete a document in the App to remove that document and its recognized text from the device. Uninstalling the App removes the App and the documents stored in its private storage.
