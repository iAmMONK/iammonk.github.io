Privacy Policy

Reaction Game (Android package com.production.monk.reactiongame). Last updated 8 November 2026.

This policy explains what the app does with information. The developer does not run a separate account server for the game.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your best reaction time on the phone. If Android backup is turned on, that value can be restored when you reinstall the app with the same Google account. The developer does not receive that score on a server of their own.

Advertising

The app shows a full-screen ad through Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK after a game ends. A game is five rounds. The ad appears after every second game, starting with the second. Loading an ad needs a network connection. The ad is separate from play. The developer does not sell personal information.

Google LLC and its advertising partners may collect:

  • the advertising ID;
  • the IP address, which can indicate an approximate location;
  • device and app information;
  • diagnostic information;
  • how you interact with ads.

They may place and read cookies, mobile advertising IDs, and similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Where you allow it, ads may be personalized. Otherwise they are non-personalized. Google may process this information outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. The policies linked below describe Google’s safeguards.

You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings (Settings, then Google, then Ads, or the equivalent on your phone). You can also manage Google ad settings at https://adssettings.google.com.

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Uninstalling the app does not delete information Google already holds. Use Google’s tools above for that.

Consent and legal bases

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app asks for your choice through Google’s User Messaging Platform before it requests ads. You can change that choice later from Ad privacy on the start screen, when that option is shown. Withdrawing consent does not affect ads that were already shown.

The best time stays on your phone. It is not sent to the developer. Advertising data is processed by Google according to the choice you give in that form, including consent where consent is required. The developer does not receive a copy of that advertising data.

Permissions

The app uses an internet connection to load ads and the consent form. It does not ask for access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files. Google Play services may read the advertising ID in order to show ads.

Sharing a result

If you share a result, the text is sent to the app you pick in the share sheet. The developer does not receive that message.

Your rights

Where data protection law applies, you can ask for access, correction, deletion, or restriction, object to certain processing, or ask for a portable copy. You can withdraw consent for ads from Ad privacy in the app, or from Google’s ad settings.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority. For this app that authority is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Alt-Moabit 59-61, 10555 Berlin, Germany, https://www.datenschutz-berlin.de.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It is not part of Google Play’s Designed for Families program. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

The reaction time

The time shown in the app is an entertainment measurement. It depends on the phone and is not a medical or scientific test.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above is the latest version.
