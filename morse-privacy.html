Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy describes how Morse Translator (the “App”), provided by Cristian Lungu, handles information. The App is a local Morse translator. It does not require an account and does not operate a backend that stores your translations.

Information processed on your device

Text you type is processed on the device for conversion, copy, share, and Morse playback (sound and vibration). That text is kept in memory while the App is open. I do not upload your translations to a server I control.

If you use Android’s share or clipboard features, those systems (and any app you share with) handle that content under their own terms.

Advertising

The App shows banner ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC) through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Loading ads and the consent form needs an Internet connection. Translation itself stays on your device.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and use:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to derive an approximate location;
  • device, app, and diagnostic information;
  • how you interact with ads.

They may place and read cookies, mobile advertising IDs, and similar technologies as a result of serving ads in the App. Where you allow it, this includes personalized ads. Otherwise ads are non-personalized.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where required. You can change that choice later from Privacy options in the App menu when that entry is shown.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, and you can manage Google ads at https://adssettings.google.com.

Google’s disclosures: https://policies.google.com/privacy, https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads, and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Permissions

The App requests Internet access to load ads and the consent form, and vibration permission for Morse playback. It does not request contacts, location, microphone, camera, or storage access.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and it is not part of Google Play’s Designed for Families program. Do not use it if you are under the age required in your country for an ads-supported app.

Retention and deletion

Uninstalling the App removes it and on-device session data from your device. It does not delete information Google or other advertising partners may already hold. Reset your advertising ID, or use the Google ads settings linked above, to limit that advertising data.
