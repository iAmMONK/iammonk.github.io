Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This policy describes how Morse Translator (the “App”), provided by Cristian Lungu, handles information. The App is a local Morse translator. It does not require an account and does not operate a backend that stores your translations.

Information processed on your device

Text you type is processed on the device for conversion, copy, share, and Morse playback (sound and vibration). That text is kept in memory while the App is open. I do not upload your translations to a server I control.

If you use Android’s share or clipboard features, those systems (and any app you share with) handle that content under their own terms.

Advertising

The App shows ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC). Loading ads requires Internet access. Google and advertising partners may collect advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and interaction data to deliver and measure ads, including personalized ads where allowed.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, a consent form is shown where required. You can change those choices later from Privacy options in the App when that entry is available.

Google’s disclosures: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Permissions

The App requests Internet access to load ads and the consent form, and vibration permission for Morse playback. It does not request contacts, location, microphone, camera, or storage access.

Children

The App is not directed at children. Do not use it if you are under the age that applies to you for this kind of ads-supported app in your country.

Retention and deletion

Uninstalling the App removes it and on-device session data from your device. It does not delete information advertising partners may already hold. Use your device’s ad and privacy settings, and Google’s ad settings, to manage advertising identifiers.
