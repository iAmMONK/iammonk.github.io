1. Scores stay on the device

The names you type for columns or players, the target, and the scores are stored only on your phone or tablet.

On Android they are stored in the app’s private preferences. On iOS they are stored in the app’s UserDefaults. We keep the current sheet and up to 12 finished games.

We do not operate an account system or a server for this app. We do not receive, and cannot read, your sheets. Starting a new game, clearing the app’s storage, or uninstalling the app removes them from the device. Your own phone backup (Google backup on Android, or an iOS backup) may include this data if you have backups turned on. That backup is controlled by you and by the platform, not by us.

2. Advertising

Belot Score shows ads through Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The banner sits at the top of every screen, in its own area, between two dividers that cannot be tapped. Loading the banner needs a network connection. Scores can be written without one.

When the banner loads, Google and its advertising partners may collect the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, diagnostics, and ad interactions.

They may place and read cookies, mobile advertising IDs, and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where that is allowed and non-personalized ads otherwise.

On Android, the advertising ID is used if the device settings allow it. Reset it in the phone settings: Settings, Google, Ads, Reset advertising ID. Google’s ad settings are at https://adssettings.google.com.

On iOS, Belot Score does not ask for permission to track you across apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency), so it does not receive the IDFA.

In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, the app asks for your choice through Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can change that choice later from the ad privacy options on the home screen, when the platform requires that entry.

We do not add our own analytics. We do not sell your information.

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
Partners: https://policies.google.com/privacy/google-partners

Uninstalling the app does not delete data Google already holds.

3. Why this processing happens

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies:

  • Storing the sheet on your device is necessary to provide the score pad you asked for.
  • The banner is how this free app is funded. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, consent for ads is requested through Google’s User Messaging Platform.

4. Children

Belot Score is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play’s Designed for Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
